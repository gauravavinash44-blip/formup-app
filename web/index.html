Ring even when your iPhone is locked

A web page can't ring while the phone is locked or FormUp is closed. Your phone's own alarm can hand you to FormUp instead:

  1. Open Shortcuts, then Automation, then +.
  2. Choose Alarm, then Is Stopped, then Run Immediately.
  3. Add Open URLs and paste your wake-up link.
