How it works

  1. At night, open FormUp, tap Start bedside mode and leave your phone on the charger, screen up.
  2. When it rings, the only way to stop it is to do your reps on camera. No snooze.
  3. Tank cheers, you earn XP and your streak grows.

Keep your ringer on and the volume up. A web page can't ring while your phone is locked or FormUp is closed, so keep it open.

Want it to ring even when locked?

Use your phone's own alarm and let it hand you to FormUp. Free and built in on iPhone:

  1. Open the Shortcuts app, then Automation, then +.
  2. Pick Alarm, then Is Stopped, then Run Immediately.
  3. Add the action Open URLs and paste your FormUp wake-up link.
